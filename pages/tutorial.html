Tutorial

Everything the board does, in one page.

Pinning a note

  1. Open the board

    Go to Noticeboard. Notes load on their own and appear newest first.

  2. Write your message

    Type into the box on the index card. You get 500 characters; the counter under the box shows how many you have used. Line breaks are kept exactly as you type them.

  3. Add your name, or don't

    The name field is optional and holds 40 characters. Leave it empty and your note is signed Anonymous.

  4. Pick the paper

    Four colours: yellow, blue, pink and green. Use the arrow keys to move between them when you are on the keyboard.

  5. Press “Pin note”

    Your note drops onto the board straight away, and onto everyone else's board too — nobody has to reload.

Good to know

  • One note every 30 seconds. The wait is remembered by your browser, so clearing your site data resets it.
  • The board shows the 60 most recent notes. Older ones stay in the database but drop off the page.
  • Times are relative — “3 minutes ago”, “last week”. Hover a timestamp to see the exact date.
  • Notes are public. Anyone who opens the page can read what you pinned, so don't put anything private on it.

Replies

Notes can be answered, but only by the person who runs the board. A reply shows up as a small slip tucked under the note it belongs to, signed Board owner.

Everyone can read replies; visitors cannot write them. If you want a note answered, that is the owner's call — the Contact page is the way to ask.

When a note is taken down by the owner, its replies go with it.

Taking a note down

A Remove button appears in the corner of every note you pinned yourself. The board recognises you by a silent, anonymous sign-in that happens when the page loads.

That sign-in lives in this browser only. Pin a note on your phone and you won't be able to remove it from your laptop — and clearing your browser data has the same effect.

If you need something taken down and the button isn't there, use the Contact page. The owner can remove any note.

If something looks wrong

The board never fails quietly. When it cannot reach the database it replaces the notes with a message saying why — no connection, daily free limit reached, or the board not being set up yet.

Reloading fixes most of it. If the same message keeps coming back, it is worth reporting.
